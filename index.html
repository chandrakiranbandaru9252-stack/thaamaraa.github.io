Why we're called Thaamaraa

Thaamara is the lotus — a flower that roots itself in the mud and still opens clean and unhurried every morning. That's the feeling we wanted for the clothes you live your everyday in.

We're based out of Telangana, and we source close to home — walking the wholesale lanes of Hyderabad ourselves, checking fabric by hand, before anything reaches our catalog. No middlemen guessing on your behalf.

We started with dresses and nightwear, because comfort should come first. Langa vonis and sarees are coming soon, once we know you a little better — your sizes, your colours, what you reach for again and again.
